On May 7th, 2026, BitDevs Malawi ran an onboarding session with the Daeyang University Computer Society. The session focused on why being part of a BitDevs community matters — from access to structured technical learning, to the value of a peer group for asking hard questions and staying accountable, to plugging into the wider Bitcoin open-source ecosystem.

We walked through what BitDevs is, how sessions typically run, and the benefits members get from consistent participation: sharper technical understanding, exposure to real protocol-level discussion, and connections to opportunities across the Bitcoin space.
